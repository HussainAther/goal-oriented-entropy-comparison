# Closely related diagnostic research

## Search scope

Targeted literature search covering:

- Goal-oriented versus norm-based error indicators.
- Predictive models of approximation errors.
- Relative entropy and dynamical response theory.
- Fisher information and perturbation sensitivity.
- Goal-dependent uncertainty quantification.

This is a targeted narrative review, not an
exhaustive systematic review.

## Birrell, Katsoulakis and Rey-Bellet (2021)

Quantification of Model Uncertainty on Path-Space
via Goal-Oriented Relative Entropy.

ESAIM: Mathematical Modelling and Numerical
Analysis, 55, 131--169.

DOI: 10.1051/m2an/2020070

### Relevance

Establishes important precedent for relative-entropy
methods tailored to quantities of interest in
stochastic dynamical systems.

### Distinction

Develops uncertainty bounds for stochastic models.

Our work compares predictive warnings for
deterministic initial-condition Taylor
approximation errors.

We do not derive path-space uncertainty bounds.

## Parish and Carlberg (2020)

Time-Series Machine-Learning Error Models for
Approximate Solutions to Parameterized
Dynamical Systems (2020).

Publisher record:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0045782520301742

### Relevance

Establishes precedent for predictive error models
using computable error indicators.

Distinguishes magnitude-based residual indicators
from goal-oriented dual-weighted residuals.

### Distinction

Our comparison uses shared first- and second-order
initial-condition perturbation information.

Our central experiment examines diagnostic rankings
under two distinct binary warning targets.

Bibliographic metadata verified against the
publisher record.

DOI: 10.1016/j.cma.2020.112990
Bibliography key: parish2020.

The paper explicitly considers normed state
errors and quantity-of-interest errors as
predictive regression targets.

Consequently, comparing different error targets
is not itself a sufficient novelty claim.
Our contribution is the specific diagnostic
geometry and controlled ranking-reversal study.

## Majda and Qi (2019)

Linear and Nonlinear Statistical Response
Theories with Prototype Applications to
Sensitivity Analysis and Statistical Control
of Complex Turbulent Dynamical Systems (2019).

Publisher record:
https://pubs.aip.org/aip/cha/article/29/10/103131/282698/Linear-and-nonlinear-statistical-response-theories

### Relevance

Uses relative entropy and local Fisher-information
expansions to assess model sensitivity and error.

### Distinction

The statistical response framework concerns
probability distributions and imperfect models.

Our diagnostics operate on deterministic positive
state trajectories and Taylor corrections.

Bibliographic metadata verified against the
publisher record and PubMed.

DOI: 10.1063/1.5118690
Bibliography key: majda2019.

The publication provides broader context for
linear and nonlinear statistical response.
Detailed mathematical comparison remains limited.

## Originality assessment

The literature establishes substantial precedent
for the individual mathematical ingredients.

Our defensible contribution is their specific
comparative computational investigation:

1. Matched-information warning diagnostics.
2. Explicit local approximation bounds.
3. Exact diagnostic ranking counterexamples.
4. Target-dependent predictive ranking reversal.
5. Prospective confirmation using fresh cases.
6. Dependence-sensitive ecological uncertainty.
7. Executable and archived computational evidence.

The targeted search did not identify an identical
comparison. This does not establish priority.

Avoid unconditional first-of-its-kind claims.
