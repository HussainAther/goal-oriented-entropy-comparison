# Literature review and scientific positioning

## Objectives

- Establish the mathematical foundations.
- Identify closely related diagnostic methods.
- Justify statistical evaluation procedures.
- Determine the scope of the manuscript's contribution.
- Avoid unsupported novelty and superiority claims.

## Review criteria

For every publication, record:

1. Verified bibliographic information.
2. Principal mathematical or statistical result.
3. Relevance to our methodology.
4. Differences from our contribution.
5. Limitations on claims supported by the source.
6. Intended manuscript citation location.
