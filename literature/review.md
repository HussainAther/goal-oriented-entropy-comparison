4. Differences from our contribution.

between goal-oriented and magnitude-based error
warnings under matched perturbation information.

The contribution combines:

- Explicit local diagnostic approximation bounds.
- Exact counterexamples to universal ordering.
- Comparative predictive performance evaluation.
- Prospectively specified target-reversal confirmation.
- Dependence-sensitive ecological uncertainty analysis.
- Reproducible computational evidence.
