# Bibliographic metadata audit

## Scope

17 bibliography entries were examined.

- 15 Crossref records retrieved.
- 1 DataCite record retrieved.
- 1 reference without a DOI (Banerjee et al.).
- No registry retrieval failures.

All DOI-bearing publication titles and years matched.
Author lists were consistent, allowing for initials
and punctuation differences.

## Discrepancies

DeLong et al. (1988): Crossref supplies the starting
page 837. The existing full page range 837--845
is retained, pending final publisher confirmation.

Majda and Qi (2019): the abbreviated journal title
was expanded to the full registry title.

## Limitations

Registry agreement establishes bibliographic
consistency, not scientific correctness or novelty.

Banerjee et al. requires direct publication-record
verification because it has no DOI in the bibliography.

The literature review is targeted rather than systematic.
Detailed theorem-level comparisons remain limited.

The retrieved registry records are retained in
registry_metadata.json.
