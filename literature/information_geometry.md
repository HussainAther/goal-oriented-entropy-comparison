# Information geometry literature review

## Nielsen (2020)

Frank Nielsen.
An Elementary Introduction to Information Geometry.
Entropy, 22(10), 1100.
DOI: 10.3390/e22101100

Full text:
https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/22/10/1100

### Established results

- Smooth divergences induce local metric structures.
- Bregman divergences admit Hessian geometry.
- Standard invariant f-divergences locally induce
  the Fisher information metric.
- See especially Equation 122.

### Relevance to our manuscript

The weighted I-divergence used in our benchmarks
is generated by the convex potential

    Phi(x) = sum_i (x_i log(x_i) - x_i) / omega_i.

Its Hessian is exactly the local metric used in
the Fisher-norm and Fisher-alignment diagnostics.

### Additional manuscript-specific derivation

For fixed positive weights, introduce an auxiliary
product-Poisson family with means

    lambda_i = x_i / omega_i.

Its Fisher information matrix in x coordinates is

    G(x) = diag(1 / (omega_i * x_i)).

Moreover, the KL divergence between the product-Poisson
distributions associated with z and X equals the
weighted I-divergence D_omega(z | X).

This establishes an exact auxiliary statistical
interpretation of the metric.

It does not assert that the benchmark dynamics or
ecological observations follow a Poisson model.

### Scientific positioning

The general divergence-metric connection is established
information geometry.

Our contribution applies this geometry to the specific
normalized warning diagnostics, derives explicit
approximation bounds, and evaluates target-dependent
diagnostic performance.

### Limitations

Nielsen (2020) is a foundational reference, not evidence
that our diagnostic ranking reversal is unprecedented.

The auxiliary Poisson representation is a separate
derivation, not a result attributed directly to Nielsen.

### Intended citation placement

- Introduction: information-geometric foundations.
- Methods: local Fisher geometry.
- Discussion: relationship to established divergence
  geometry.


## Banerjee et al. (2005)

Arindam Banerjee, Srujana Merugu,
Inderjit S. Dhillon, and Joydeep Ghosh.

Clustering with Bregman Divergences.
Journal of Machine Learning Research,
6, 1705--1749.

https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume6/banerjee05b/banerjee05b.pdf

### Established results

Section 4.3 develops the correspondence between
regular exponential families and regular
Bregman divergences.

### Relevance

Provides established theoretical context for
the auxiliary product-Poisson representation
of our weighted I-divergence.

The reference is already present in references.bib
under the key banerjee2005.

### Auxiliary Poisson derivation

For independent Poisson variables with means
x_i / omega_i and fixed positive weights:

    score_i = y_i / x_i - 1 / omega_i

    Var(Y_i) = x_i / omega_i

    Fisher_ii = Var(Y_i) / x_i^2
              = 1 / (omega_i * x_i)

Cross-information terms vanish by independence.

Thus Fisher information equals the Hessian
of the weighted Bregman generator.

The KL divergence of the auxiliary product-Poisson
distributions equals the weighted I-divergence.

### Scope

The Poisson construction is an auxiliary
statistical representation, not an observation
model for the benchmark systems.

The exponential-family and Bregman correspondence
is established mathematics, not a novelty claim.

The manuscript-specific contribution concerns
the comparison of normalized error warnings,
controlled local approximation bounds, and
empirical target-dependent diagnostic performance.
