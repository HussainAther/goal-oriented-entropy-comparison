# Source data and reuse

Dataset: Wojcik et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.51c59zwj5; publisher: Dryad. Dryad policy states datasets are CC0 (https://datadryad.org/help/guides/reuse). The exported posterior draw CSVs are derived from the authors' fitted R posterior objects. Cite both the dataset and the research article.

Our Python analysis, metrics, and figures are derivative research outputs; they do not imply independent biological forecasts. The upstream R-to-CSV posterior conversion and Stan refitting are not executed in this package. Check copyright/attribution for original scripts separately; no authors' original R code is re-licensed by this package.

Public release of analysis code: assign an explicit license only after the author approves.
